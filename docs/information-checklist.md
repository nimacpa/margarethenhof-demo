### A. Decisions from the project owner (Nima)

- [ ] Is this a working system the family will actually use, or a portfolio prototype with sample data? (Decides how Phase 4-5 are built.)
- [ ] Have the family/owners been told about the project, and do they agree?
- [ ] Who will maintain the site after launch (menu changes, closing days, bookings)?
- [ ] Budget for domain, hosting and any paid services (even small monthly costs).
- [ ] Target launch date, if any.

### B. Business basics (family)

- [ ] Final website name and spelling of the address
- [ ] Phone number and email (confirmed)
- [ ] Opening hours: restaurant, kitchen and bar; holidays, closed days, Betriebsferien
- [ ] Social media links, Google Business profile, existing website, existing reviews
- [ ] Logo (original file) and any brand colours
- [ ] The story: history, family, what makes the place special, the Persian-German idea
- [ ] Owner/operator legal name and address (for the Impressum)

### C. Menu

- [ ] Latest menu (file or photo) and the date it is valid from
- [ ] Confirmed prices
- [ ] Allergen and additive information per dish
- [ ] Vegetarian / vegan / halal notes
- [ ] Dishes the family wants to highlight, plus daily or weekly specials
- [ ] Kitchen hours and last order times
- [ ] Who translates dish descriptions to English (or approval of a draft)
- [ ] Do they want a separate children's menu, group menus, or event menus?

### D. Floor plan and seating

- [ ] Floor plan of every guest area: drawing, sketch or photo from above, with rough measurements
- [ ] List of all tables: number/name, seats, position, shape
- [ ] Which tables can be joined together, and for how many guests
- [ ] Areas (Stube, Saal, Theke, Terrasse/Biergarten if it exists), what each is called, and the seats in each
- [ ] Reserved-only vs. walk-in tables; the Stammtisch
- [ ] Special seating: window seats, quiet tables, high chairs, wheelchair access, dogs allowed
- [ ] Total seating capacity inside and outside
- [ ] Photos of each area from a few angles

### E. Reservation rules (the board's key questions)

- [ ] Typical time guests stay (by party size, e.g. 2 people vs. 6 people)
- [ ] Booking slot interval (every 15 / 30 / 60 minutes?) and the first and last bookable time per day
- [ ] Friday/Saturday late-night rules (dinner slot vs. drinks only)
- [ ] Minimum and maximum party size online; groups above that by phone only
- [ ] How far ahead guests can book, and how late (e.g. not within 2 hours of the slot)
- [ ] Share of tables kept for walk-ins
- [ ] Can guests pick a specific table, or only an area?
- [ ] Who confirms a booking, how fast, and by what means (email, phone, WhatsApp)?
- [ ] Automatic confirmation, or manual approval for every booking?
- [ ] Cancellation rules, late arrival (how long is a table held), no-show policy
- [ ] Deposit or credit card requirement (usually not for a Gasthof; confirm)
- [ ] Special occasions: birthdays, celebrations, whole-restaurant bookings, events
- [ ] Who sees the bookings during service, on which device (phone, tablet, printout)?
- [ ] Which email address receives requests, and who watches it, including on Ruhetage?
- [ ] Information guests may be asked for (name, phone, email, allergies, occasion, high chair)

### F. Pension / Ferienwohnung

- [ ] Pension, holiday apartment, or both? How many units?
- [ ] Confirmation of 6 guests, 3 bedrooms, 4 beds, 1 bath
- [ ] Description, amenities, size, floor, kitchen, parking, Wi-Fi, pets, accessibility
- [ ] Photos of every room, bathroom, kitchen, entrance and the building
- [ ] Prices, minimum stay, cleaning fee, tourist tax (if any)
- [ ] Check-in / check-out times, house rules, cancellation policy
- [ ] Booking method: Airbnb only, direct enquiry, or own calendar? Role of the Airbnb listing

- [ ] Local tips (what to do in Bueren-Brenken) for the English audience

### I. Technical (the assistant handles these once A-H are answered)

## Priority Order For Asking The Family

Ask in this order so the family is not overwhelmed and the project can keep moving:

1. Floor plan and table list (D), real photos (G) - these gate the most unique feature.
2. Latest menu with prices and allergens (C).
3. Contact details and opening hours (B).
4. Reservation rules (E) - best discussed in a short conversation, not by form.
5. Pension details (F).
6. Legal details (H).

A family-friendly German version of this list should be created as a separate short document (and ideally
a short conversation, not a long form).
